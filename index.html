<# Privacy Policy **Synapsil** Last updated: May 20, 2026 Contact: privacy@synapsil.app --- ## 1. What We Collect ### Account Information When you create an account, we collect your email address and name via Clerk (our authentication provider). ### Health & Fitness Data (Apple HealthKit) With your explicit permission, Synapsil reads the following data types from Apple HealthKit: - Heart Rate Variability (HRV / RMSSD) - Resting Heart Rate - Sleep Analysis (duration and stages) - Step Count - Active Energy Burned This data is transmitted to our servers solely to calculate your personalized daily calorie targets and recovery score. **We never use your HealthKit data for advertising, user profiling, or sale to third parties.** ### Nutrition & Training Logs Food entries, meal photos, macro targets, and workout data that you manually log inside the app. ### Subscription Data Your subscription status and transaction history, processed by RevenueCat. ### Usage Data Anonymous app usage patterns (screens visited, features used) to improve the product. No health data is included in usage analytics. --- ## 2. How We Use Your Data | Data | Purpose | |---|---| | HealthKit metrics | Calculate daily calorie target and recovery score | | Food logs | Track nutrition against your targets | | Account info | Authenticate your account and sync across devices | | Subscription status | Determine your access tier (Free vs. PRO) | We do **not** use your data to: - Show you advertisements - Sell your data to third parties - Build advertising profiles - Share with data brokers --- ## 3. Data Storage Your data is stored on servers hosted by Fly.io (United States) using a Neon PostgreSQL database. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. Health data sent to our servers is stored with your user account and is used exclusively to provide Synapsil's recovery scoring and calorie personalization features. --- ## 4. Third-Party Services | Service | Role | Privacy Policy | |---|---|---| | Clerk | User authentication | clerk.com/legal/privacy | | RevenueCat | Subscription management | revenuecat.com/privacy | | Anthropic (Claude) | AI meal photo analysis (PRO feature) | anthropic.com/privacy | | Fly.io | Cloud hosting | fly.io/legal/privacy-policy | | Neon | Database | neon.tech/privacy-policy | When you use AI photo meal logging, your meal photos are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for macro estimation. Photos are not stored by Anthropic beyond the API call. Synapsil does not retain your meal photos after analysis. --- ## 5. Apple HealthKit — Additional Disclosures In compliance with Apple's HealthKit guidelines: - HealthKit data is used **only** to provide Synapsil's core features (calorie targets and recovery score) - HealthKit data is **never** used for advertising or user profiling - HealthKit data is **never** shared with third parties except as required to provide the service (stored on our servers) - You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Synapsil --- ## 6. Data Retention We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all personal data including your health records and food logs are permanently deleted within 30 days. --- ## 7. Your Rights You have the right to: - **Access** all data we hold about you - **Export** your data in a portable format - **Delete** your account and all associated data - **Correct** inaccurate personal information - **Revoke** HealthKit permissions at any time in iPhone Settings To exercise any of these rights, email **privacy@synapsil.app**. --- ## 8. Children Synapsil is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. --- ## 9.
